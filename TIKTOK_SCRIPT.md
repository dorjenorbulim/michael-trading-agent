## **Editing Notes**

### **Pacing:**
- Cuts every 2-3 seconds max
- Use jump cuts on terminal
- Speed ramp: slow on trade entry, fast during analysis
- Sound effects on every trade

### **Visual Effects:**
- Typewriter effect for commands
- Green flash on profitable trades
- Red flash on losses (if any)
- Glow effect on active strategy cards
- Particle effect on dashboard updates

### **Music:**
- 0:00-0:15: Build up / tech beat
- 0:15-1:15: Medium energy, driving
- 1:15-2:15: High energy, drop
- 2:15-3:00: Triumphant, resolve

### **Captions:**
- Large, bold, high contrast
- Sync with voiceover
- Emoji reactions to trades
